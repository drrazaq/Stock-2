How this page's calls have turned out

Read only. This page cannot place orders and holds no broker login. Everything you enter is saved only on this device.

Long only, daily candles. Each stock gets its own rule, chosen from 72 versions of two ideas: buying a moving average cross, or buying a dip inside an uptrend, each with or without a market filter. The choice is scored by walk-forward testing: at four points in the past the page picks the best rule using only earlier prices, then counts how it did on the years that followed. A Buy is shown only if the rule made money on those unseen years, in at least 3 of the 4 periods, without depending on one lucky trade. It re-learns whenever new prices arrive, and pauses a stock whose last four finished calls lost money.

Past results do not predict future results. This is a mechanical rule, not financial advice.
