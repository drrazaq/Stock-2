Long only, daily candles. A buy is a moving average cross upward while price is above the 200 day average. An uptrend means price above the 200 day average, the 50 day average above the 200, and a gain over 6 months. For each stock the page tests 27 versions of the rule on older history, picks the best, then checks it on recent years it has not seen. It repeats this every time new prices arrive.
