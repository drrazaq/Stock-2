Long only, daily candles. Each stock gets its own rule, chosen from 108 versions of four ideas: a moving average cross, a dip inside an uptrend, a new 52 week high, or a 20 day high on double volume, each with or without a market filter. Every test charges trading costs. The choice is scored by walk-forward testing on years the rule had not seen. A Buy watch appears only if the rule made money there in at least 3 of 4 periods, did not depend on one lucky trade, and beat simply holding the S&P 500 over the same days. A rule in use is replaced only when a challenger is clearly better. Thinly traded stocks, stocks marked not halal, and buys beyond 5 positions or 2 per sector are blocked. News, analyst ratings, insider activity and earnings dates are context only.

Past results do not predict future results. This is a mechanical rule, not financial advice.
