## Exact source contract exercised offline

Three originals from the already allowed Alpaca single-symbol bars route, all explicitly `feed=sip&adjustment=raw`:

1. Current opening/reclaim bars: the declared minute interval, exactly contiguous from the supplied regular-session open through the last completed decision bar. Native OHLCV, volume × native VWAP, actual response-receipt availability, original row/body hashes and derived IDs are retained. The last completed interval must precede the receipt and decision. No interpolation or close-price substitution for VWAP.
2. Comparable prior panel: the same interval, spanning the preceding 20 supplied calendar sessions. Every required session/offset must be present once. The median uses exactly 20 corresponding volumes, including zero-volume rows where valid; a zero median remains UNKNOWN. Other historical bars are retained in the original and cannot substitute for missing offsets. DST changes follow each supplied session open rather than a fixed UTC offset. Missing sessions are never dropped from the denominator.
3. Prior daily panel: exactly the same 20 prior dates, requested from the first native daily timestamp through the last prior session close. New York date mapping preserves daily timestamps separately from market close. Every prior session must have closed before the receipt, and the receipt must precede entry-session open. Liquidity is the arithmetic mean of the 20 native daily volume × VWAP values. This is **aggregate-derived dollar liquidity**, not a claim to all traded dollar turnover or complete market coverage.

Pagination must be explicitly complete, prices/volumes finite and valid, rows strictly ordered and unique, query fields unambiguous, symbols/routes exact, and all source clocks within the existing acquisition and decision bounds. A partial/late/conflicting response, missing comparable row, wrong security, adjusted source, future bar, invalid VWAP or inconsistent supplied field stays UNKNOWN. A role conflict remains recorded and cannot be cleared by restart or by choosing a preferred original. No retries, expanded deadlines or new acquisition allowance are introduced.

## Full original-input assembly and strict evaluator

`derive-entry-inputs` extends the same `runAFirstCaptureStep` caller. Its pinned request supplies already-retained originals through `nativeBinding.entryInputs`; it never calls a provider or invents a missing original. The new original-reader uses the same signature/role verification as strict evaluation, with no default roots or real keys.

- Calendar: exact paired CAPTURE/WITNESS originals of the documented Alpaca trading-calendar response, plus an independent completeness/time-zone review. Regular/early-close times convert from New York to UTC without assuming a fixed offset. The first regular session after the original reviewed publication and its preceding session are derived from that calendar; a complete 20-session history and five-session runout plus next session are required. Recognizing a retained calendar URL adds no calendar GET route to the bounded client.
- Prior market cap: exact paired retained reference originals for the matching FIGI/CIK and prior date, positive USD market cap and a reviewed prior-close basis. Crucially, the original response must actually have arrived after the prior close and **before the event publication**. A retrospective `date` parameter, a new response after the event or today's shares/price cannot satisfy this. A reviewer binds the cap original and its as-of basis before entry.
- Agreement: the existing timely independent REVIEW original must bind the actual primary document hash, stable security, fixed committed USD amount and the derived prior cap/session facts. Software does not manufacture legal/economic claims from a keyword or treat a signature as source truth.
- Owner: derive the latest owner assertion actually known by entry, require a nonconflicting valid interval and retain its original receipt. A latest NO remains NO; an old YES cannot override it. No mark is written, new YES generated, 90-day NO exclusion changed or proposed validity policy adopted.
- Identity and quote: derive the issuer/evidence identifiers and common-stock type from the existing native identity review and originals; derive the one bound native entry quote through the existing normalizer/quality contract. Ambiguous quote candidates remain UNKNOWN rather than choosing a favorable quote.
- Bars and panels: derive the fields described above and retain the complete per-field origin table. Equivalent timestamps normalize to canonical UTC in derived fields; original strings, nanosecond timestamps, raw body hashes and actual clocks remain intact.

Freeze rederives the full candidate, requires exact decision equality and stores its origin table inside the immutable entry-facts original. Evaluation requires that exact assembly again, including original/reference/role hashes: changing a prior original after freeze, even to the same numerical value, cannot silently replace provenance. The strict native evaluator independently rederives these fields, so a direct importer cannot bypass them. Retained-research native input now requires the full bindings; old synthetic structural rehearsals remain separately labelled and do not acquire missing history.
