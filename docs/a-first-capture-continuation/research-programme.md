### A — material commitment, intraday reclaim, multi-session repricing

Exact rule and finite30-entry/60-calendar-day proposal: [A checkpoint](a-hypothesis-checkpoint.md), [original protocol](../a-catalyst-reclaim-pilot/protocol.proposed.json). Pre-open fixed committed USD agreement≥10% prior cap; first following session; price≥USD2, prior20 dollar liquidity≥USD500,000, spread<3%; first completed5-minute volume≥1.5×same-interval prior20 median; completed pullback strictly holds opening low then completed close reclaims opening high. Entry after that trigger with quote/trigger age≤30seconds and displayed capacity, expires with the new trigger's30-second limit and session close. Earlier of fifth-session close or next open after an earlier close below opening low; no intraday stop/target. USD1,000 whole-share/$3-side/ask-bid/0.25%-side view plus unchanged required stresses. No pure day-trade exit invented.

Mechanism: slow interpretation of enforceable information and residual demand; falsifier: no timely remaining executable move after costs or no positive excess against either frozen control. Amount/cap is not surprise, margin or NPV. [Frazzini–Israel–Moskowitz](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Working-Paper/Trading-Costs-of-Asset-Pricing-Anomalies) documents how costs differ across anomaly classes and finds short reversal especially fragile; it is institutional evidence, not evidence for A. No located primary study establishes this exact commitment/reclaim rule. No authentic A return sample exists here.

Needs primary agreement/earliest dissemination and independent timely review, pre-event cap receipts, prior panels/calendar, genuine turnover, owner state, all possible entry/exit BBO windows and full rejected population. Small names amplify spread, dilution, ceiling-award and halt risk; exact20 panels do not prove coverage semantics. Software binding now works; aggregate volume×VWAP fails closed for genuine inputs because constituent trade sets can differ. **Data failure is not a strategy loss.** First useful authentic question is one predeclared chronological attempt including rejection/zero events and all exit runout; that cannot estimate an edge.
