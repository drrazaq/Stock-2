## Fixed limits and atomic admission

Proposed logical budget: **4 open checkpoints, each at most32MiB of locally measured unpublished reachable Git objects; at most128MiB conservatively summed across four slots**. This deliberately lowers the old proposed24-ref capacity; it does not change a running setting. One slot reserved forbot, one formain, two shared by the five other existing writers. Bot/main each have at most1open checkpoint; all other writers together have at most2. No option-product research is authorized by including existing writers in storage inventory.

Use four fixed reservation refs in a separate namespace `refs/heads/r1-recovery-slot/{bot,main,shared-0,shared-1}`. A reservation points to the exact checkpoint tip. The actual checkpoint retains the reviewed unique immutable `save-recovery/<writer>-<run>-<attempt>-<UTC>-<sha>` name. Before any attempt, verify the existing publication guard and calculate the checkpoint's uncompressed object size against the captured localmain reference; if size or guard is UNKNOWN or exceeds32MiB, do NOT publish. Capture main/ref identity in the sanitized receipt. A stale-but-monotonically advancingmain gives conservative local accounting; history rewrite would invalidate that assumption and must stop admission.

Create reservation AND checkpoint in **one atomic push**, both with an expected-absent lease. No force overwrite, no unconditional fallback. Example structure (proposed operator code, not an instruction to execute):

`git push --atomic --force-with-lease=<slot>: --force-with-lease=<checkpoint>: origin <tip>:<checkpoint> <tip>:<slot>`

A remote slot already claimed causes the entire push to fail, including checkpoint creation. An UNKNOWN listing is not permission to bypass the cap: atomic expected-empty creation is the authority. At most one attempt per allowed slot for one state, within existing retry/deadline bounds. Main/bot have one slot; others have at most two candidates. Unsupported atomic pushes, unexpected ref state, local-size uncertainty or ambiguous acknowledgement fail closed. Do not create another checkpoint on an ambiguous result; reconcile the exact name/tip pair read-only when available. No automatic retries outside reviewed save deadlines, forced replacement, borrowing another role's reserved slot, extra storage route or deletion to make room.

The slot count, not an eventually consistent count query, enforces the cap across runners. Four slots times32MiB bounds the conservative **logical unpublished payload admission**, with zero concurrent-admission overshoot, provided the reviewed local32MiB guard and role/ref rules are implemented. Objects shared by checkpoints may be counted repeatedly; this may exhaust early but never invents extra capacity. Main advancement/merges do not authorize another admission until the relevant slot is explicitly released.

## Retention, custody and exhaustion

A slot has no automatic expiry. Unmerged checkpoints are never evicted or deleted. Health may label PENDING from7days and STALE from30days using the checkpoint name's creation clock. That clock is not commit time or trustworthy server arrival time. Malformed/future/contradictory clock means ageUNKNOWN and no deletion; retain first independently observed time as a separate diagnostic when available. No age promise substitutes for custody.

After an exact data-only recovery has actually reachedmain and passed all restoration checks, the already-reviewed merged-checkpoint cleanup may be extended to delete its checkpoint AND associated slot together, atomically, with leases on both exact tips. Never release a slot merely because a merge was attempted or an acknowledgement was unclear. A racing/new tip makes cleanup fail without deleting either. Stale unmerged deletion still needs separate approval naming both checkpoint and reservation names/tips after authorized semantic reconciliation; ciphertext diff alone cannot prove commands/offsets were recovered. Unknown state means keep both. No deletion is executed now.

Custody remains this same repository's guarded ciphertext, decryptable only under the reviewed owner-held recovery/key arrangement. Reservations point to the same objects and add no plaintext payload. Owner-held recovery outside the runner remains a separate prerequisite; no key is requested or enrolled here. Recovery remains UNVERIFIED and no Enable until required complete receipts and every restoration gate pass.

**Exhaustion/L7:** ifmain cannot accept the save and the permitted slot(s) are occupied/refused/unconfirmed, state may exist only on the runner and be lost when it ends. Preserve existing retries/deadlines and loud failure/UNKNOWN handling; never report a confirmed save or tell the owner to replay financial commands blindly. Four slots do not provide a time guarantee: bot's one slot can be occupied by its first failed state; the next run30minutes later may have no slot, and distinct states can arise sooner.
