## 1. Common measurement contract

For each immutable security ID, source event ID/revision, rule/version and session, record these clocks independently in UTC with original timezone, precision, source and clock uncertainty:

| Clock | Meaning | Required treatment |
|---|---|---|
| t_event | Original publicly available event time; for a price rule, first contemporaneously observed qualifying trade/bar | A filing date is day precision, not an invented midnight publication. A revised story retains both original and revision times. Unknown stays unknown. |
| t_provider | Provider dissemination/availability time, if supplied | Separate contractual delay from network/system delay. Missing provider time does not make the source instantaneous. |
| t_ingest | First system receipt of that original event/revision | Preserve across restored jobs. A report regeneration or repeated fetch cannot reset it. |
| t_actionable | First observation satisfying the frozen rule AND the prespecified quote/identity/eligibility checks | Also record the earlier research-lead time, every suppression, and why actionability was later or absent. No later quote backfills this clock. |
| t_delivery | Telegram API ACK time for the exact logical message and all parts | Separate request start/end, message IDs, partial/UNKNOWN/reserved outcomes and any owner-reported receipt. ACK is not proof of phone receipt or reading. No actual delivery exists in the proposed collection experiment. |

Report t_ingest−t_event, t_actionable−t_ingest, t_delivery−t_actionable and t_delivery−t_event separately, p50/p95/tails and missing-clock counts. A negative/contradictory clock invalidates latency credit. Day-only sources have interval-censored latency; report bounds, never exact seconds. Until real delivery is separately approved, measure a labelled **would-send preparation time**, not t_delivery.

At the last eligible quote BEFORE the source event (when genuinely observed), at first ingestion, actionability, delivery and each frozen horizon, record bid/ask, sizes, trade/quote/volume timestamps and source/feed. Report price change already completed using an explicitly named reference (event-preceding midpoint, previous regular close, and session open separately). Report subsequent bid-versus-entry-ask opportunity, adverse excursion, horizon after-cost return and time to observed +5/+10/+20/+25. An observed maximum is descriptive, not an exit price; missing intervals cannot prove a continuous target hit. Halts/gaps can jump over a target.

Freeze the requested universe and event/rule eligibility BEFORE outcomes. Retain all screened symbols, non-signals, late signals, losers, conflicting/revised sources, rejected executions and unresolved exits. After each day, find missed runners in that same prespecified universe using the fixed +10% intraday close-reference crossing and +25% within10-session study labels; these are **diagnostic outcome labels**, never an entry-selection rule. Distinguish NO_SOURCE, NOT_REQUESTED, NOT_RETURNED, STALE_QUOTE, UNKNOWN_IDENTITY, RULE_NOT_TRIGGERED, LATE_DETECTION, HALT and DELIVERY_UNKNOWN. Report full requested/received/fresh/actionable/delivered denominators by session, price, liquidity, issuer size and listing venue. A missing/uncertain market cap is its own stratum, not a removed small cap.

Massive Starter is nominally **15-minute delayed**. Its lead time is measured against the original event clock, with delay retained; it cannot earn a real-time discovery pass. A fresh SIP quote confirming a delayed lead is **fresh confirmation of delayed discovery**. Broad Alpaca SIP and extended-hours coverage remain unverified; the prior189/200 regular-session sample and isolated fresh quotes do not establish whole-market surveillance.

## 4. Minimum evidence, uncertainty and frozen stopping rules

**Historical discipline remains five years** where suitable data exists: point-in-time representative universe including delisted/renamed/share-class/corporate-action and terminal events; separate small/large-cap, sector, price, venue, liquidity and market-regime coverage; registered entry/exit/hold/stop/version; fixed older/newer splits with purge/embargo at least the maximum forward hold; untouched confirmation; realistic costs/capacity; same-date industry/price/liquidity controls and SPY total return; concentration, drawdown, stress and removing2 largest wins. Preserve every original frozen firstRun, rejected/unproven rule and separate labelled corrections. Re-running daily-cache studies is not authentication of that history. Five calendar years alone provide few independent long-hold regimes.

Daily grouped caches cannot reconstruct minute-level catalyst availability, premarket depth/halts, intraminute order of target/stop, queue/fills or first-delivery timing. Today's active list is not a historical survivor-complete universe. Without an independently licensed/as-known intraday archive, those intraday historical claims remain **NOT ESTABLISHED**. Do not purchase data or infer missing paths; use a bounded prospective route instead.

|---|---|---|

These additions supplement every original rule-specific frozen acceptance gate, luck bar, stress test and rejection; they cannot replace a stricter requirement or promote a rejected/tier-2 rule to proven. These are **floors, not statistical proof or universal power guarantees**. Example planning calculation: detecting50bp mean edge with2% independent-case SD,90% power and a conservative two-sided family-adjusted critical value≈2.77 for9 primary comparisons requires approximately ((2.77+1.28)×.02/.005)^2≈263 independent observations before clustering. Real day/issuer correlation, fat tails, multiple horizons and missingness increase this;300 raw cases may have far fewer effective observations. For long holds, overlapping cohorts cannot be counted as independent trades. Estimate nuisance variance/design effects in a separate blinded/calibration cohort, freeze sample-size/end-date rules before seeing evaluation returns, and do not combine that pilot with confirmation.

Before enrollment, hash the protocol, rule/parameters, source and runtime/workflow, input/universe snapshot, ZAD/admission, fees/size/capacity, entry/exit/stop and holiday/half-day policy, matched-control algorithm, all primary/secondary endpoints, family of comparisons, confidence method, censoring and end date. Use date/security-clustered inference; for medium/long overlapping holds use prespecified time blocks at least the holding dependence span, with joint portfolio paths. Prespecify Holm familywise α=.05 (or stricter reviewed simultaneous intervals) across every claimed lane/horizon/session; a winning secondary endpoint cannot become the primary. Confidence bounds must use the same family adjustment. Report cash-weighted net returns and path drawdowns, not a selective average of independent $1,000 wins.

At the fixed end:
- **PASS for that aim/cohort only** if all agreed timing, coverage, tradability, cost/edge/confidence, concentration and drawdown criteria pass in evaluation AND untouched confirmation, with ≥95% eligible paths resolved. Report all missing paths; perform prespecified adverse missing-outcome sensitivity. If plausible missing losses overturn success, no pass.
- **FAIL** if sufficiently observed data rejects an agreed economic/risk/latency condition; retain negatives, not just significant winners.
- **NOT ESTABLISHED** for insufficient cases, missing identity/clocks/control/quotes, wide adjusted confidence intervals or incomplete followup. No automatic extension until results improve.
- One issuer may contribute at most10% of positive aggregate P&L and one industry at most30% (proposal); otherwise report concentrated/inconclusive and test removal, never delete cases to pass.
- Stop an individual collection run immediately on scope/clock/entitlement/authentication, encryption/storage, request budget or unauthorized endpoint failure. Preserve incomplete evidence. Stop a paper risk cohort at its agreed drawdown boundary; retain the stopped cohort as a risk failure, no fresh reset to erase losses.
- Stop enrollment at the frozen date even if minimums are unmet; finish existing followups only within its approved scope. A changed rule/threshold/source starts a new version/cohort, preserves the old result and requires renewed approval for any extra collection. No peeking-triggered success stop or count-based optional stopping. More observation may confirm failure or remain inconclusive.
