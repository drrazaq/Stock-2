# A dated operating-profit predicate, not a scored recovery trade

This resolves one source-feasibility question using a named lead from the completed Micron investigation. It does not repeat the archive search or acquire prices. The existing proposed recovery rule, its DEEPNODIL universe/depression/dilution conditions and 20-session exit remain unchanged. No qualifying trade or ZAD eligibility is asserted.

The original [20 March 2024 SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/0000723125-24-000004-index.htm) was accepted at 16:00:45 New York (20:00:45 UTC). Its [original earnings exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312524000004/a2024q2ex991-pressrelease.htm) supplies standalone GAAP operating income, in USD millions: Q2 FY2024 +191, Q1 FY2024 −1,128 and Q2 FY2023 −2,303. The [original September 2023 exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312523000051/a2023q4ex991-pressrelease.htm) supplies Q4 FY2023 −1,472 and Q3 FY2023 −1,761. These are consecutive standalone quarters; no annual/cumulative differencing or non-GAAP substitution is needed. The immediately preceding four quarters are nonpositive, followed by a positive quarter. This does not mean the preceding loss streak was exactly four quarters long.

The filing arrived after the regular close. Under the already proposed rule, that close cannot be used; eligibility is assessed only at the next session close, then entry is the following session. Filing acceptance is a public availability witness, not the earliest global news timestamp or an original bot ingestion receipt. No historical price path, entry, fill or outcome was inspected to select this specimen.

The positive GAAP number does not establish durable operating improvement. The same original exhibit discloses $382 million of lower costs from sales of inventory previously written down and a $200 million patent cross-license gain; adjusted free cash flow was −$29 million. Subtracting only the inventory benefit from operating profit gives −$191 million as a diagnostic, **not** reported earnings, a new filter or a restatement. These facts expose a weakness in the proposed economic proxy before any trade scoring: first GAAP profit can reflect accounting effects while cash generation remains weak. We do not retroactively exclude this case to manufacture a stronger rule.

**Completed:** a five-quarter original-disclosure chain and a dated public filing witness are recoverable for one illustrative issuer. **Still blocked:** authentic contemporaneous price/depression and share-count eligibility, the full PIT universe and denominator, known-then ZAD eligibility, original ingestion, executable entry/exit/actions/halts and matched controls. This single selected example is not an unbiased population or evidence of underreaction. The bounded recovery hypothesis remains INCONCLUSIVE; rejected DEEPGAP/DEEPEARN/DEEPNODIL and unproven pullback rules retain their verdicts. No new strategy family, acquisition or live restriction follows.
