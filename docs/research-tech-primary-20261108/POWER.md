## Distinctness and terminal research result

The economic bottleneck is credible as a business-assessment mechanism: delayed grid access can make deliverable onsite power or licensed incremental nuclear output valuable. The shareholder mechanism still depends on price, contract obligations, capex, incentives, timing, regulation and execution. An “AI demand” association is not enough. Operational risk resolution can differ from the rejected customer-EPS supplier-drift rule, but it overlaps the already identified new-commitment/qualification uncertainty mechanism. Do not create a separate strategy family or revive SQ28 by calling it AI power.

These cases improve stock assessments now by identifying exactly what changed and what remains an intention. They do not establish post-announcement underreaction. All selected cases have known narrative outcomes and are development examples, including a regulatory setback; no hit rate or mean return is estimated. Missing: untouched event denominator, original availability/expectation evidence, stable PIT eligibility, ask/bid/condition/time/displayed size, halts/actions, exact integer entry/exit, fees and common controls. No entry/expiry/exit/holding rule is retrospectively fitted to these examples.

**INCONCLUSIVE on trading economics.** One existing distinct-mechanism source-feasibility study may be worth independent challenge; these additions do not justify another parallel programme or a data purchase. Any broader event population must be enumerated independently of outcomes before prices, include revisions/cancellations/adverse rulings, and retain unchanged controls and all$1,000 whole-share/$3-side costs.
