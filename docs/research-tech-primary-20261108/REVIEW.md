## Mechanism, falsification and useful result

A genuinely different potential mechanism would require a newly disclosed, economically enforceable increment to supplier cash flows or a resolution of material product-qualification uncertainty, followed by incomplete immediate incorporation into price. Customer EPS/returns or an existing theme/link alone do not supply that mechanism. MU demonstrates why prior disclosure matters; AVGO why existing relationships and replacement commitments matter; GTAT why financing, purchase minimums and downside obligations matter. These are practical corrections to a discovery lead, not evidence of post-cost underreaction.

Falsification includes no incremental change versus the prior public record, no enforceable customer demand or credible cash-flow consequence, immediate incorporation leaving no executable residual after costs, or performance no better than unchanged customer/supplier controls in an untouched sample. No price data were inspected, so the last two economic questions remain unanswered. A later rise cannot establish an available entry, an achievable exit or a profitable whole-share trade.
