## Implemented / missing / contradictory

|---|---|---|
| Form 4 purchase selection | IMPLEMENTED, narrower claim needed | Exact document type `4`, non-derivative code `P`, acquisition `A`, positive shares/price. SEC code P includes open-market **or private** purchases; these fields alone do not establish open-market execution or personal cash. Footnotes, ownership and consideration are not retained. |
| Several executives buy | CONTRADICTORY wording | Library E3 says “executives”; code counts reporting owners, including potentially directors, 10% holders and entities. ## One minimal primary hypothesis — draft only

**Conditional hypothesis:** among predeclared eligible US common stocks, a cluster first verified by this system of **three distinct, unambiguously attributed reporting-person CIKs** reporting positive-priced, non-derivative P/A acquisitions in the inclusive filing-date window above has positive mean **paired net return** against one pre-entry matched non-cluster control, from the next regular-session opening observation after verified availability to the close of the **20th session counting entry as 1**. This tests reported purchases, not “executives spending their own cash,” transaction-date clustering, causal impact, or whole-market coverage.

Use one primary count/window/horizon; no BIG1, two-person, executive-only, transaction-date, 60-session, stop or target variants. Retaining the closest existing count/window avoids a new threshold search; the historical NOT PROVEN result remains adverse prior evidence. The new question is whether authentic timely, point-in-time evidence supports that specific condition; a new name does not reset the multiple-testing history. No assertion that it merits trading or will succeed.

Proposed enrollment: first authenticated crossing as known at system receipt; at most one enrollment per verified security in the following 30-calendar-day cooldown. Only filings already accepted and actually ingested may contribute. Deduplicate accession/transaction/owner evidence, not just ticker/day. Missing earlier observation history blocks a first-crossing claim; retain it as uncertain research context. Use the next session after the later of verified acceptance and actual complete system receipt (New York calendar), never backdate entry to a filing date missed by the system. Freeze the matched control before entry: same date, eligible universe, industry, price/liquidity and size bands, all selected from pre-entry data, with deterministic tie-breaking and no known cluster. Apply the same applicable identity, ZAD and executable-quote rules to both legs; cluster exposure itself is the intended difference. No suitable control means explicitly unmatched, not a replacement chosen after returns are known.

Required data before a separately approved study:

- Immutable original filing/XML and acceptance-response bytes, issuer/security identity, accession, form type, owner CIK and role/ownership fields, transaction row/date/code/shares/price, footnotes, authentic acceptance time and original system receipt/proof-ready clocks. Preserve source routes, hashes, rejected events, repeats and all missingness. Joint/entity ownership requires attribution rather than an assumption of three independent executives.
- Append-only accession/revision lineage including 4/A and original links. An amendment seen before entry may change current eligibility only as of its receipt. One seen after entry adds a dated annotation/followup; it cannot remove the earlier enrolled case, replace its evidence/entry, or retrospectively supply a missing third person. Unknown amendment linkage remains unknown. Preserve an as-known-at-signal view and a separate latest-corrected view.
- Point-in-time common-stock/share-class, industry, size, eligibility/ZAD and corporate-action reference history; prior-session price and 20-session dollar-volume history (existing research screen: price ≥$3, ≥15 valid observations and mean dollar volume ≥$1m). Universe must include eligible losers/delistings, with explicit missing coverage. No ticker-only identity joins.
- Time-aligned entry ask / exit bid, sizes, source/feed and quote clocks for event and control; whole-share capacity, fees and additional slippage. Quote-quality PASS is not a guaranteed fill. Do not add spread again when ask/bid already includes it. The old 0.8%/1.2% modeled costs remain historical assumptions, not newly measured execution costs. Halts, gaps, missing quotes, dilution and unavailable exits require fixed missingness/outcome treatment; retain every enrollment and do not substitute an earlier close as its scheduled exit.
- A pre-registered session/date range, request/storage bounds, fixed enrollment/end rules, power/sample-size and uncertainty plan, matched-control distance/tie rules, missingness/cost/quote thresholds and stopping rules **before collection**. Keep calendar splits, concentration and winner-removal checks; they must not be used to fish for a replacement primary hypothesis. No arbitrary new pass threshold is approved here.

Alpaca Plus and Massive Starter ownership is confirmed; protected SIP endpoint access/quote-age behavior is not established, and Massive Starter remains 15-minute delayed. Authentic timely Form 4 observation, receipt history, reference joins and the paired 20-session lifecycle are implementation/data dependencies; quarterly caches cannot backfill them. This would be a separate proposed research protocol, not adoption or alteration of frozen V1/V2/30-minute capture. Next step, only if later prioritized, is independent review of this single hypothesis and a bounded data/collection scope; execution needs separate approval.
