# Stock-2 opportunity-first redesign: audit, Phase 1 build, plan (28 Oct 2026)

**Owner decision (28 Oct):** approved four Phase 1 investigations:

1. shadow-test every soft filter against winners and losers;
2. five-day outcomes in the alert log;
3. catalyst verification inside alerts;
4. bid/ask spreads and realistic execution costs.

Everything that would change *which* stocks reach the owner (ranking, A/B/C, any rule) is a **proposal below, awaiting approval**.

## 9. Approvals requested (nothing below is built)

1. **A/B/C shadow ranking (Phase 3, shadow only, frozen on approval).**
   - Same stream: hard-eligible candidates meeting EW-ALERT-1 between 9:35 and 11:30, with the live SEC check and catalyst done.
   - Each system decides at the candidate's trigger minute (no look-ahead) and takes at most 5 a day.

   | System | Picks |
   |---|---|
   | **A, existing** | First come, first served (today's behaviour) |
   | **B, opportunity-first** | Catalyst VERIFIED or UNCONFIRMED, spread 1% or less, day move +6% or less (room left), 2M USD+ traded |
   | **C, simple baseline** | Time-of-day volume pace 3x+ and spread 1% or less |

   - Scored identically: entry at the ask, 6 USD fees; same-day, 1- and 5-session net; median, without 2 best, worst, daily 5 × 1,000 USD portfolio drawdown; vs all eligible candidates and SPY.
   - Verdict after 20 sessions. **Only sessions after the approval date count.**
2. **Switching the live alert ranking** to B or C. Only if 1 shows it better on all of average, median and without the 2 best; a separate approval at that time.
3. **Historical minute replay (Phase 2):** a Saturday job using Massive minute bars to rebuild 9:30 / 9:35 / 9:45 / 10:00 candidate states for past sessions. Its size and Massive plan limits need checking first.
4. **Repository privacy (Part 11):** see 10.
5. **Scheduler:** only if the 10-session watcher record fails its bar. An external scheduler (free, needs a token outside GitHub) or a ~5 USD/month server.

**Recommendation:** (a) + (b) now. (c) only with a cheaper bot host.

## 11. WHAT COULD WE BE WRONG ABOUT?

1. **The shadow test inherits the early-wave lane's blind spots.**
   - It only sees candidates between +2% and +15% with a volume pace, and IG triggers.
   - A filter that matters outside that window (gaps, quiet drifts) is not tested. The `chgMax` gate is only tested up to +15%.
2. **Quotes are not fills.**
   - The ask at the snapshot can be seconds old (age recorded). Under IEX the quote is one exchange.
   - Exits at the close assume half the entry spread.
   - Real fills for thin stocks can be much worse; the one-minute-delay variant only partly covers that.
3. **Small samples.**
   - 10 sessions in one market mood (late-October small-cap strength) can make a filter look useless or essential by chance.
   - Verdicts need 30+ rejected candidates and median plus without-2-best agreement, and are re-checked as data grows.
4. **The catalyst check is shallow.**
   - SEC acceptance time is solid. A Finnhub headline is an aggregator's timestamp, not the original source.
   - "No catalyst found" does not mean "no catalyst".
   - Contradictory evidence is not assessed.
   - The shadow rows have no catalyst, so catalyst-based ranking (B) can only be tested on trigger-met candidates.
5. **Defining A/B/C now may still encode hindsight.** B's thresholds (6%, 1%, 2M) come from judgment, not data. They are fixed before any outcome exists, and only post-approval sessions count. If B wins, it still needs an independent confirmation period before going live.
6. **The guard and the cron may still miss mornings.** The first proof is tomorrow's `watcher-starts.jsonl`, not this document.
7. **The goal itself:**
   - A favourable distribution of +2% net short-term outcomes may not exist in the data this system can see in time.
   - 744 tested ideas say short-term chasing mostly loses after costs.
   - The honest end state may be "the radar is useful for awareness, not for trading". The pre-registered numbers are designed to say so if it is true.
