## Actual producer and request routes

Production base is SEC hotfix `bd70f5a`. The loop obtains market snapshots and evaluates ignition/early-wave paths before `pollEdgar`, then `decideFilings`, then controls. EDGAR polling loads a cached ticker map if needed and pages existing Atom 8-K/6-K responses. Polling is about every110seconds until11:00NY, first scan up to800entries per form and later scans up to200, in100-entry pages. Atom `updated` currently supplies the producer's `atM`; it is not separately verified SEC acceptance.

`decideFilings` waits until a minute after this producer timestamp, at least09:30. It needs a usable fresh price before attempting the cheap D checks. Those checks include form/items,04:00–11:00producer timestamp,09:30–11:00decision, price>=1, prior-day dollar volume>=1M, price change within−3%/+3%, applicable fresh-spread check, ZAD/lottery and SEC exclusions. A missing/stale quote or pending minute can defer processing; events with no usable price can remain queued through11:00. Only a cheap-D-pass with no per-symbol `EA.S.sec` cache entry requests issuer proof; the D route caps such calls at15per minute and keeps excess queued. **This is not a global cap on all SEC calls.**

Other existing routes include `maybeAlert` (history/paper/rule, ZAD and fresh-price gates), early-wave catalyst checks through the existing `secRisk` callback, and the11:15radar's per-symbol checks. Each can obtain/cache issuer submissions under different price/selection conditions. `EA.S.sec` is keyed by symbol; the D/radar code uses a truthy cached result without an explicit TTL there. Failed/unknown results can also remain cached and prevent another D request. Do not treat a cache hit as a new proof request, or assume it contains the current accession. The ticker map is separately cached in `TICK`. The exact existing routes are `GET https://www.sec.gov/files/company_tickers.json`, `GET https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?...output=atom`, and `GET https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK##########.json`; the deployed helper bounds each SEC request at10seconds. The native tee adds zero calls.

The current native response archive records URLs, exact bodies and receipt/persistence clocks, but does **not** establish all caller-route, cache-hit/miss/vintage, queued-budget or per-event no-request reasons. A shared submissions response must not be assigned a route merely from approximate timing. Some SR filing decisions preserve D prices/reasons, but queued/no-price/no-request events are not a complete pre-entry decision ledger. These are required V2 telemetry inputs, not fields to backfill from later prices or responses. A separately reviewed observational instrumentation patch would be needed; it is not installed by this package.

## Distinct proposed cohorts and clocks

* **A — proposed primary, first observation in-window:** the original first native stream observation is within09:30–09:34NY. Record SEC acceptance separately. A filing accepted premarket but first ingested after the open is delayed discovery in A, not a premarket first observation. The proposed primary analysis is restricted to a documented existing D proof-request route (or its documented D-origin cache), with all applicable pre-entry D predicates and symmetrically eligible controls observed before entry. Other routes are retained in separate labelled strata, not pooled into the primary cohort.
* **B — premarket first observation reconsidered at the open:** original ingestion/observation occurred before09:30. Preserve those clocks. Reconsideration/proof readiness at the open is a different event; it must never be relabelled as first observation in A. B is a separate proposed secondary calibration stratum, with its own gaps, price move already completed and eligibility clock. It is not untouched confirmation of A or V1.

For either reviewed V2 stratum, `eligibilityEstablishedAt` would be the first persisted moment when the original event, matching existing submissions proof, route/cache provenance and required pre-entry conditions are all available. It cannot precede their original receipt clocks. No source timestamp, first ingestion or original observation is replaced. Eligibility and entry must meet the separately frozen window/expiry; take only the next fresh executable BBO after eligibility and timely handoff. Record price movement from acceptance to observation, from observation to proof readiness and to actual paper entry. No retrospective quote or entry is allowed.

## Controls and every-event accounting

Controls must face the same applicable pre-entry selection and proof-opportunity conditions as candidates, including identity/ZAD, price/quote/size/liquidity and the documented D-route price/caching predicates. The current quiet-stock R0 sample at09:45 is **not** an equivalent control for price-selected SEC-proof candidates and cannot be silently substituted. The minimal proposed primary comparison is among symmetrically eligible SEC-event risk-set members, matched using only pre-entry information under separately agreed rules. This estimates conditional relative performance; it does not isolate the causal effect of a filing. If no eligible control exists, retain the lead as `NO_SYMMETRIC_CONTROL` and do not admit a primary pair. SPY remains a benchmark, not a substitute matched control. Any changed control rule requires independent protocol review; V1 controls stay unchanged.

Retain every original event and revision, including no proof request, price-gate failure, missing/stale/unavailable quotes, nonqualifying form/items, missing identity/ZAD, already-moved price, cached failure, cache without accession, cache provenance unknown, per-minute request-budget deferral, source error/rate limit, outside window, premarket cohort, missing symmetric control and unresolved history. Distinguish `NO_REQUEST` from `REQUEST_FAILED` and genuine empty results. Unknown reasons remain `REASON_NOT_RECORDED`, not reconstructed market explanations. Preserve losers, exclusions, queue delays, retries (if any existing producer route made them) and complete quote followups; no selection by later outcome.
